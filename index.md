# STREAPEX Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 24 October 2026  
**Last updated:** 24 October 2026

STREAPEX (“STREAPEX”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is a fitness, activity-tracking, and personal-wellness application. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the STREAPEX mobile application (the “App”).

If you have questions or want to make a privacy-related request, contact us at **streapex14@gmail.com**.

## 1. Information we handle

The information handled by the App depends on the features you use, the permissions you grant, and the configuration of the services integrated into the App.

### Account and profile information

When you register or maintain an account, we may handle:

- Your email address.
- Your name or display name.
- Your profile picture, if you provide one or choose to use one.
- Account identifiers and account status needed to operate your account, including email-verification status.

STREAPEX uses Firebase Authentication for account-related functions. If email verification is required, Firebase may process your email address and authentication information to send verification messages and help secure sign-in.

### Fitness, activity, and app-progress information

Depending on the features you use, STREAPEX may process information such as:

- Step counts and related pedometer information.
- Activity records and distance or exercise information, where those features are used.
- Focus-session records and focus history.
- Daily tracker entries and progress information.
- Reminder settings and information needed to schedule or deliver reminders.

Some information may be generated by your device or entered by you. The exact data fields stored, and whether each item is stored locally, in Firebase Cloud Firestore, or in another service, depend on the App's implementation.

STREAPEX is not intended to provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Do not rely on the App as a substitute for professional medical advice.

### Information handled by advertising services

The App may display banner advertisements and rewarded advertisements using Google AdMob. To serve, measure, limit, or protect advertising, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device and app information, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad interaction information, and fraud-prevention signals, subject to the applicable Google services, your device settings, consent choices, and regional requirements.

STREAPEX does not intentionally collect precise GPS location for its own location-tracking feature. However, third-party SDKs may process IP address or other technical signals that can be used to infer approximate location. Check the current AdMob configuration and Google's disclosures for the actual data practices that apply to this App.

You may be able to reset or limit your device's advertising identifier through Android settings. Doing so may affect advertising personalization, but it may not stop all advertising or all processing needed to deliver ads.

### Diagnostics and usage information

Firebase and other software development kits integrated into the App may process technical information to operate their services, prevent abuse, understand service reliability, or diagnose errors. Depending on which SDKs and options are enabled, this could include app events, crash reports, device information, performance data, and diagnostic logs.

We have not confirmed whether Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics is enabled in the current release. This section must be checked against the actual build and Firebase console before publication; this policy should be updated if the App collects these data.

## 2. How we use information

We may use information described in this policy to:

- Create, authenticate, maintain, and secure user accounts.
- Verify email addresses and help prevent unauthorized account access.
- Provide profile, steps, activity, focus, daily-tracking, progress, and reminder features.
- Save and synchronize information where the relevant feature uses cloud storage.
- Display and operate banner and rewarded advertisements.
- Respond to support messages and privacy requests.
- Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App, where applicable.
- Detect fraud, abuse, security incidents, and technical problems.
- Meet applicable legal obligations and enforce our terms or protect rights and safety.

We do not describe the App as collecting a category of information merely because a service could collect it. Actual collection depends on the features, permissions, SDKs, and service settings enabled in the released version.

## 3. Where information is stored and processed

STREAPEX uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore where configured for account and app data. Google and its service providers may process information on servers located in different countries, subject to their service terms and safeguards.

Some activity or app information may be stored on your device; other information may be sent to cloud services if the App is implemented to do so. The precise division between on-device and cloud storage should be confirmed from the current source code and Firebase configuration.

We do not intentionally request precise location permission for location tracking. Android and third-party services may still process technical information, such as IP address, as described above.

## 4. When information may be shared

We do not sell your personal information in exchange for money. Information may be processed or shared in the following circumstances, as applicable:

- **Firebase / Google:** To provide authentication, cloud storage, security, and related services used by the App.
- **Google AdMob and advertising partners:** To request and display ads, measure ad performance, prevent invalid activity, and provide advertising-related services.
- **Service providers:** With providers that help operate, secure, or support the App, only as needed for their services.
- **Legal and safety reasons:** Where disclosure is reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal process, protect users, investigate fraud, or protect rights and safety.
- **Business changes:** If the App or its assets are involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer, subject to appropriate protections and applicable law.

The privacy terms and practices of Google and other third-party providers also apply to their own processing. Review Google's relevant privacy information and advertising controls for further details.

## 5. Permissions and notifications

STREAPEX features may need access to device capabilities or permissions—for example, activity-related sensors or notification permission for reminders—depending on your Android version and the implementation of the App.

You can review or revoke app permissions in Android Settings. Revoking a permission may prevent the related feature from working fully. The App's actual permission declarations and runtime requests should be checked before this policy is finalized.

## 6. Retention of information

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App's features, maintain account security, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, and enforce applicable agreements.

Retention periods may differ by data type and service. We have not specified a fixed retention period because the actual storage locations, backup practices, and deletion behavior must be confirmed from the App's implementation and the relevant Firebase and AdMob settings.

## 7. Account deletion and privacy requests

To request account deletion or ask about your personal information, email **streapex14@gmail.com** from the email address associated with your STREAPEX account, if possible. Include “STREAPEX – Privacy Request” in the subject line and explain your request. Do not send your password.

We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law and may ask for reasonable information to verify the requester's identity.

Deleting an account should not be assumed to automatically remove every item held by every service. Before confirming deletion, we will need to identify the App's account-deletion process and ensure that associated Firestore records and other relevant data are handled appropriately. Some information may be retained where legally required or for legitimate security purposes, as permitted by law.

**Implementation note for the publisher:** This policy currently reflects that users contact support to request deletion. Ensure that requests are actually handled and that any applicable Google Play account-deletion requirements are met, including any required in-app and web-based deletion paths.
